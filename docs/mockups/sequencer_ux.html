A dedicated step row between the transport and the lanes. Steps carry name + length chip + resize grip (whole-Q snap, cycle-multiple default; ⌥ free with ⚠ badge). Lanes subdivide at step boundaries; clicking a lane×step span toggles that gate in place — you gate the bass where the bass lives.

Recommendation — A + B are one control at two depths

The strip (A) is the focused stack's sequence — always the root when you're at the top; dive into a group (ZUI) and the strip becomes THAT stack's sequence. The pad grid (B) is the in-place editor for any stack you haven't dived into — the seq · NQ chip on its rail expands it, exactly like the fx row. Same object, two densities; C folds into A. Gate toggling works in both places (lane spans and pads) because both are pure projections of one gates map.

Interaction grammar (proposed, reusing the existing vocabulary): click cell/pad = toggle gate · drag across pads = paint · step grip drag = resize (whole-Q snap, cycle-multiple default, ⌥ free + ⚠ badge + frame-health badge when a sibling LCM would blow up) · double-click step header = rename · double-click strip gap = split step · right-click step = heal/merge (the cut-band grammar) · ⟲ loop on a step = the S9 window (audition; arm a track while it loops = Mode-2 record into the step, S17) · chip click = bypass toggle (the jam comes back).
