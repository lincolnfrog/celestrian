2. Not a control — what the LANES do when a sequence is active

This is the part of old "Variant A" that survives, and it was never optional: the period law says an active sequence makes the stack's period the song (28Q), so the session view — a pure projection — must re-frame to it. Lanes tile their loops across the song with gated-off spans dimmed; the one playhead sweeps it; the ruler picks up step ticks. You read the song here; you edit it in the grid. One edit surface, no duplicate control.

3. The S17 flow — "record the solo over the breakdown"

The step header's ⟲ loops that step: it sets a one-segment window over the song (the S9 composition law — same object as any loop region, cyan brackets on the lanes). While it loops you're auditioning. Arm a track and record: through-map recording into the step, take capped at the step length, commit auto-gates it ON here, OFF elsewhere. Undo removes take + gates as one edit.
